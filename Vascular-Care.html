Vascular Care Treatment

VASCULAR SURGERY - deals with the blood vessels of the body except the heart and brain. Blood vessels undergo age related changes and also blocks due to many conditions hampering the blood flow to the different parts of the body particularly to the hands and legs. Such disturbance results in severe pain and eventually gangrene if not treated in time.

Once gangrene is set in the tissues will not serving leading to removal of that part. Varicose veins cal cause a series of ulcers, infections and complications if not treated in time. These specialists can remove clots, connect them with most modern grafts to reestablish the blood flow.
